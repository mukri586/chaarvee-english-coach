# Content and safety guide

The AI content generator is designed for an approximately 13-year-old Grade 8 learner in Ontario.

## Preferred subjects

Science, technology, environment, history, geography, space, animals and nature, arts, music, sports, inventions, community, culture, learning, literature, ethics and everyday decisions.

## Avoid

Sexual content, graphic violence, self-harm or suicide, drugs, profanity, pornography, gore, horror, extremist propaganda, partisan political persuasion, and mature relationship themes.

## Reading design

Generated passages should be original and should not reproduce or closely imitate copyrighted articles. The system generates its own passages and questions rather than scraping third-party sites.

## Educational progression

Grade 8: clear thesis, paragraph structure, evidence, basic analysis.

Grade 8+: stronger reasoning, inference, vocabulary and evidence integration.

Grade 9: counterarguments, nuanced reasoning, textual analysis and synthesis.

Grade 9+: increasingly complex passages and multi-step analytical writing.

AP/IB Foundation: interpretation, authorial choices, evidence-analysis links, synthesis and precise academic writing.

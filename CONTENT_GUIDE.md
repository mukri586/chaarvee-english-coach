# Age-appropriate content guide

The starter library is written for Grade 8–9 learners. It avoids sexual content, graphic violence, self-harm, drugs, profanity, and other mature themes. Topics emphasize science, environment, community, technology, learning, literature, and everyday ethical questions.

When adding AI-generated reading material, keep the same standard. Prefer school-appropriate informational and literary content. Avoid asking the model to reproduce copyrighted articles; use original passages, public-domain material, licensed content, or links to permitted sources.
